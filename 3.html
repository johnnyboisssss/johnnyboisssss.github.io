What We Need

October 19, 2026

What do we need as a society? We need knowledge. Knowledge is the means by which every conscious organism flourishes. Without knowledge, all conscious life would die out nearly immediately. What do we need to know? What are the most important things people need to understand to improve their lives and, by implication, those of the rest of humanity?

How to Know

For most of human history, people have had no clear idea about how they can know the world. They are unsure of the method by which to acquire knowledge, and so they either deny the possibility or pick some mangled pseudo-epistemology. The metaphysical analogue of these alternatives are subjectivism and intrinsicism. The denial of knowledge generally comes from the belief that the mind is the creator of reality; that truth is dependent on the mind of the person perceiving it. On the other hand, mystic, rationalist, and other similarly impoverished epistemological stances deny the mind as the identifier of reality. A man, missing a proper method of reasoning, attempts to know reality by magic, floating logic, or some other means which violates the identity of his mind. The proper epistemology is the objective one: the one that includes the mind and reality. The objective approach to epistemology is detailed in books like How We Know and Understanding Objectivism. To better understand objectivity, it helps greatly to learn the proper theory of perception.

Direct Realism & Ecological Psychology

Reality is real. Existence exists. It is specifically what it is independently of what people pretend that it is or wish it would be. You cannot wish that an apple were a kilogram of gold. If you wanted to change it into gold, you'd need to perform a complex, adaptive process requiring a great deal of thoughtful action. You must actually perceive reality to identify what this process would entail. You'd have to look at the apple, differentiate it from gold, form the concepts required, identify general principles to guide your action, engage in a deductive process to choose between alternatives, etc. Ecological Psychology is the science of direct perception. I believe that the insights of ecological psychologists are revolutionary for the field. It's something about which I know very little and am eager to learn more.

Selfish, Objective Ethics

The Objectivist theory of ethics is based on the facts of life. Everybody already understands it implicitly. They say, "if your goal is to jump higher, you should do train your ability to jump." "If you want to use a private currency, you should use Monero." Objectivist ethics says, whatever it is that you want to do, there are objective requirements for living as a human. We do not thrive by no means. We do not mindlessly pursue our goals. We must use reason to identify what we must do to flourish or we will fail to achieve anything we want. Maybe the worst cancer pervading the philosophical canon is the ethical doctrine of altruism. Until a man grows out of it, he will hate himself for failing to live up to the impossible standard he's set for himself, and he will hate every man that is productive and self-serving. If he does learn, he will succeed and take joy in seeing others do the same, because he knows they can only have done so per their own choice to be rational.

Anarcho-Capitalist Legal Theory and Austrian Economics

The culmination of Objectivist metaphysics, epistemology, and ethics is a legal theory. What principle defines when one may initiate conflict? The firstcomer principle: The firstcomer should win the conflict. That means that the person who has excluded the other from a good should win against the person who has overcome that exclusionary border. For example, the person who built a fence around a piece of land is right to exclude someone jumping over that fence to run across his land, and that trespasser should not have done so. The application of this legal theory would cause unimaginable success for humanity. We know this is true for many reasons explained in detail by the Austrian School of Economics, which is based on Praxeology. The Austrian School is the correct school of economics.

Humans should, by and large, avoid cooking their food, eating non-fruit plant matter, and mostly anything other than the foods I've mentioned previously.
