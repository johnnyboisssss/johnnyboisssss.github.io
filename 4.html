One Must Imagine Sisyphus Miserable

October 23, 2026

As punishment for your trickery, you have been immured in a personal, custom realm. You see a rock and a large mountain. They're mocking you with their obvious purpose. You begin to roll the stone up the hill, wondering what will happen when you reach the top. Eventually, after a grueling effort, nearly at the top, you slip, or a piece of the hill breaks beneath you. You watch as the rock rolls all the way down the hill. You must begin the slow walk back down. You try again. You fail again. You try. You fail. You hope for a while that you will succeed--that after you complete this task there might be someone to talk to. Slowly, you begin to realize the nature of your punishment. You begin to look for a way out of this prison. You try every trick you can imagine. You simply cannot accept the suffering you know you'd feel if you gave up.
