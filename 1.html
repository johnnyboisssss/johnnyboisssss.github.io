My Inspiration

I was inspired to write this blog by a few people. Instead of naming them, I'll simply state my reasoning and my intentions for this blog. Firstly, I want to write to improve my ability to check my ideas. I want my arguments and thinking to be grounded in perception of reality. Writing is a process of formalized thinking. It is a concerted effort to bring one's implicit, automatized ruminations into explicit clarity. The second reason for writing this blog is to refine my articulation. Expressing one's ideas in a digestible, essentialized way is how you win people. Being concise and flowing in your speech and writing is, in my opinion, the best indicator of intelligence. I want to demonstrate that to people in my regular life. Writing a blog, as opposed to simply writing in a journal, gives one the sense that his writing will be read by another. Each step must be fully enunciated. Nothing can be left vague or rationalized. You must convince another who does not believe you. This is how one learns to be persuasive.

So how do I want this blog to operate? What procedure do I plan to enact to get the most out of this? First, I should write firstly to find the truth, but also to persuade. This way, I'll need to show you why each step is justified. If I haven't articulated it at least once before such that I can reference that article, I will need to show that it is true. Not only that, but I'll need to continuously check my premises. If an idea is too dusty, I'll spiral on it and check it. Secondly, I want to edit each article down so that it is concise. The first draft is always long and awful. The edited version, with sentences and paragraphs rewritten and reorganized, will be far better than the original. It should convey all the same points in way fewer sentences, while keeping the same persuasive power. I also want to practice my ability to concretize. I want to use colorful examples that help me remember what I've written and use the points I've made in reality. This is how humans think. I want to exercise my ability to be an objectivist rather than a rationalist in my valuing. I don't want to begin with categories and try to fit them. I want to write this blog with a flow of emotion and without dutiful restrictions. I want to write what I'm inspired to write.
