My Inspiration

October 12, 2026

Why do I want to write this blog? I want to write to improve my ability to check my ideas. I want my arguments and thinking to be grounded in perception of reality. My second reason for writing this blog is to get better at articulating in a simple and effective way. Being concise and flowing in your speech and writing is, in my opinion, the best indicator of intelligence. I want to demonstrate that to people in my regular life. Writing a blog is different from something like a journal because you are showing your process to a hypothetical reader. Each step must be fully enunciated. Nothing can be left vague or kept rationalized. You have to convince someone who does not already believe you. This is how one learns to be persuasive---how one learns to win people.

What will I do to get the most out of this blog? Firstly, I'll attempt to demonstrate why my ideas are true. To do this, I'll need to continuously check my premises. If an idea has gone stale, I'll spiral back on it. Secondly, I want to be concise. I want to cut, rewrite, and reorganize my sentences and paragraphs, while keeping the same essential epistemological steps. I also want to practice my ability to concretize. I want to use examples that bring what I'm thinking about to life. This is how humans think. Finally, I want to follow my emotions; I want to write about what's meaningful to me. Trying to live categorically is the easiest way to destroy your inspiration.
