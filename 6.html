It's Never Over

November 5, 2026

Looks matter

They matter a lot. They are perhaps the most important thing for achieving success in romantic relationships. If you look good, you will never struggle to find romantic connection. The power you hold in a relationship depends primarily on your looks. This is the most important realization of black pill philosophy. The pain that arises from the knowledge that what you knew about women, romance, and life was wrong leads to some interesting rationalizations. This article will describe some of them.

Genetic determinism.

You are ruled by your genetics. You don't have any control over your life. Everything that you do is just the inevitable product of the roll of a set of genetic dice. To validate this belief, you'd need to choose the truth over falsehood. You'd need to be able to direct your consciousness toward thought rather than evasion. If you accept that this is impossible, then you accept that your belief is invalid. Determinism in all its forms is self-defeating.

Only looks matter

This is untrue because romantic relationships are not the only meaningful value one may gain, and looks are not the fundamental means by which one attains them. If you want a relationship, looks are the most important thing. They are the fundamental requirement (excluding rationality) for success in that area. But, if you want to eat good food, enjoy a walk in the forest, lay in the sun, or make friends, you can find success. Men will be much more forgiving of your looks. Many men find company with other bad looking men. You can make each other better with time. In some areas of life, looks can even be slightly detrimental. For instance, in highly technical fields, people will assume you got to where you are by looks alone and evaluate your skills as lower than they actually are. A reverse halo effect is possible because the philosophy and value judgments are the cause of the emotional reactions of people.

Looks cannot be improved (It's over). This is untrue because it is always theoretically possible that we find a new way of improving our looks. Imagine that one day, humanity invents a way to seamlessly change the structure of the human face. Perhaps nanobots become able to slowly morph ones osseous features over weeks whilst a person's regular functioning resumes undisturbed, or maybe we discover a way to program the behavior of osteoblasts and osteoclasts such that they grow bone in specfic shapes and directions. I personally call this possibility apotheosis. And even now, there is a lot someone can do to improve their looks. Plastic surgery gets more advanced every day. If we were to reach an anarcho-capitalist society, it's very possible that most or all productive people could look amazing. At that point, your face and height would simply be an expression of your aesthetic choices and judgments. This is all to say that looks are very much not an exception to the power of the human spirit. Armed with reason, we can improve our circumstances--the universe is there for us to discover.

A is A

It is never over. The world is what it is, and if we adapt to it, we can succeed. We can achieve our values. We must discover what to do and how to do it through reason.
